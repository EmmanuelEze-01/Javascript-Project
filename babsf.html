Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry

Prep Time 20 min
Cook Time 15 min
Serving 4
Difficulty Medium
Calories Per Serving 380 Kcal
Ingredients:
  • Beef sirloin, thinly sliced
  • Broccoli florets
  • Soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Sesame oil
  • Garlic, minced
  • Ginger, minced
  • Cornstarch
  • Cooked white rice for serving
Instructions:
  • Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil and cornstarch.
  • Stir-fry the beef until browned, then remove it.
  • Stir-fry broccoli, garlic and ginger in the same wok.
  • Return the beef to the wok and add the sauce.
  • Stir until everything is coated and heated through.
  • Serve over cooked white rice.
